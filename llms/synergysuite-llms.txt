# SynergySuite

> Back-of-house restaurant management platform for multi-unit and enterprise
> restaurant operators (founded 2011; US, UK, Ireland). Covers inventory and
> purchasing, recipe and food costing, food safety/HACCP, labor scheduling,
> cash management, HR and business intelligence. SynergySuite operates a real
> production integration surface — POS integrations, EDI and data services are
> separately monitored components on its status page — but publishes NO public
> API documentation, reference, or machine-readable contract. API access is
> distributed through a partner portal that requires a login.
